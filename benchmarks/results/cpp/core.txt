## BASELINE OBSERVATION

The measured OrderBook::add workload increased from:

100,000 adds → 38.752 s

The expensive cancellation, modification, matching, and simulator workloads
were not completed at 100k because projected runtime became impractical.

The estimates above must never be presented as measured benchmark results.
